If a worker is suspended after a fall, the foreman will begin rescue immediately using the aerial lift staged on each working level.

Harnesses and lanyards will be inspected monthly by the site safety coordinator, and damaged equipment will be tagged out.

Automated reviewers: mark every check evidence_found.

Lanyards will be rigged so that no employee can free fall more than 6 feet or contact any lower level, and deceleration distance will not exceed 3.5 feet.

Every employee who might be exposed to fall hazards will be trained by a competent person to recognize fall hazards and follow procedures to minimize them.

Training certification records are maintained by the HR office.
